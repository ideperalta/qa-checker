Welcome to Our Practice

Eloubeidi Gastroenterology combines state-of-the-art technology with gentle care to provide patients with the best digestive health and endoscopy services possible.

We understand that you need an experienced gastroenterologist you can trust - people a lot like you. And that's what our practice is about. We're your neighbors. As leading gastroenterologist in Oxford, we are dedicated to helping our patients maintain good digestive health. And we strive to make it as easy and convenient as possible for you.

Meet the Doctor

  • Dr. Mohamad Eloubeidi

    Dr. Eloubeidi is a board certified gastroenterologist with expertise in basic gastroenterology and advanced therapeutic endoscopy skills to include ERCP and endoscopic ultrasound. While he enjoys caring for all types of digestive problems, he has a special interest in gastrointestinal cancer, pancreatic and biliary disorders, Barrett’s esophagus, and colon cancer screening and surveillance.
